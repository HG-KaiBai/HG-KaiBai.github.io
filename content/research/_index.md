## R Packages & Web Interfaces

### mortselect

**Mortality forecasting with data-driven selection and ensemble methods.**

I am an author and the maintainer of **mortselect**, an open-source R package developed with Liqun Diao, Yechao Meng, and Chengguo Weng. It implements deletion-substitution-addition (DSA) population selection, age-specific band selection (ASB), and bivariate model-based ensemble (BMBE) forecasting, with configurable age ranges, study periods, and forecast horizons.

The accompanying **Mortality Forecast Explorer** lets users compare saved forecasts by age, population, and year through interactive charts, with CSV and SVG downloads. It runs in the browser and displays precomputed results; new model fitting and forecasting are available through the R package.

[R package & source code](https://github.com/HG-KaiBai/mortselect) · [Open web interface](https://hg-kaibai.github.io/mortselect/)
