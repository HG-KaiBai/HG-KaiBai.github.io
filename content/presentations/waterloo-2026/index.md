##### Title

Neural Regression Trees for Censored and Uncensored Outcomes

##### Author

Yixing Zhao, Liqun Diao

##### Abstract

Estimation of the full conditional distribution is considered for exact or censored continuous outcomes. Nonparametric regression allows this distribution to vary with covariates without imposing a fixed parametric form. A Neural Regression Tree (NRT) is proposed for this purpose. The response is partitioned by a complete binary tree with a fixed topology on a bounded time window. For each covariate vector, split locations and left-branch probabilities at all internal nodes are determined by a neural network. Products of the branching probabilities along each path give normalized terminal-node masses. A uniform density within each terminal interval then defines a valid conditional density, distribution function, and survival function. The validity of this construction is established. As the tree and network complexities increase, continuous conditional distribution functions on compact domains can be approximated uniformly under the stated assumptions. Simulation studies and real-data analyses are used to evaluate estimation and prediction performance in uncensored and mixed-censored settings.
